# Sounds

The seven key clicks in `sfx/` are single keystrokes from **Keyboard Soundpack #1 (Typing and Single Keystrokes)** by unicaegames, released under **CC0** (public domain): <https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes> (archive `unicae_games_keyboard_soundpack_1.zip`, folder `Single Keys/`; recorded on a Cherry KC 1000).

Each was converted to 48000 Hz mono 16-bit PCM, trimmed so it starts on its click, brought to a peak of about 0.8, cut to 0.25 s and faded out over its last 50 ms.

The other five sounds (`pop`, `whoosh`, `swap`, `chime`, `tick`) are generated by `src/sfx-synth.mjs`.

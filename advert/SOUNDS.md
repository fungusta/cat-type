# Sounds

The seven key clicks in `sfx/` are single keystrokes from **Keyboard Soundpack #1 (Typing and Single Keystrokes)** by unicaegames, released under **CC0** (public domain): <https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes> (the page lists the archive as `unicae_games_keyboard_soundpack_1.zip`; the download is named `unicae_games_keyboard_soundpack_1_0.zip`; folder `Single Keys/`; recorded on a Cherry KC 1000).

Each was converted to 48000 Hz mono 16-bit PCM, trimmed so it starts on its click, given a gain that brings its peak to about 0.8, and cut to at most 0.25 s (the files are 0.18–0.24 s long). Where a file runs past 0.2 s it fades out from 0.2 s to its end; the shorter ones (`key-6`, `space`) are not faded.

The other five sounds (`pop`, `whoosh`, `swap`, `chime`, `tick`) are generated by `src/sfx-synth.mjs`.

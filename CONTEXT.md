# Cat Type language

## Product

- **Cat Type** — the desktop companion that appears while someone types.
- **typing companion** — the animated cat experience; it reacts to keyboard activity and fades after typing stops.
- **cat style** — one of the six visual cats: gray, ginger, charcoal, brown-tabby, white, or black-and-white.
- **wardrobe** — the collection of equippable accessories that customize every cat.
- **achievement** — a permanent reward earned through typing activity or active days.

## Website intent

- **new user** — a person discovering Cat Type who needs to understand the charm quickly and reach a platform download.
- **website visual direction** — the landing page shares the app's toybox language: neutral surfaces and dark ink, rounded shelves and pill actions, with Butter Sun reserved for the primary action and unlocked or selected things.
- **product proof** — real Cat Type artwork and believable app details that show the experience rather than relying on generic marketing imagery.
- **homepage promise** — a cute typing companion that brightens your day; then three supporting promises, each in its own **feature band**: customize the cat, unlock new clothing and accessories, and track your typing.
- **primary download** — the main landing-page action, which matches the visitor's computer and downloads that platform's file directly; every other offered platform stays one click away. The landing page offers Windows and Mac only, for now.
- **feature band** — one short section of the landing page devoted to a single supporting promise, carried by one picture and one sentence.
- **motion approach** — the hero cat performs on its own using the existing flat cat artwork; visitors watch rather than type, and the page does not need three.js or a 3D scene.
- **wave** — a landing-page gesture: the cat lifts one paw and waves to greet the visitor and invite them to download. It is not one of the app's typing poses.
- **starter item** — a visible accessory earned within about a first week of ordinary use: at most 10,000 keystrokes or 7 active days. Secret rewards are never starter items. Only starter items appear in **Mix it up** on the landing page.
- **typing activity** — what the Track feature band describes: keystroke counts by hour and day, never the text itself. The landing page says it shows your most productive times, but never scores productivity or tracks focus, tasks or goals.
- **unlock showcase** — the Unlock feature band: locker-style tiles for one earned reward and a few locked ones with progress. Secret rewards are neither shown nor hinted at.
- **customization interaction** — visitors choose a cat style and press **Mix it up** to vary the outfit; they do not manage individual accessories on the landing page. The cat they dress becomes the hero cat.
- **copy voice** — calm product language with occasional dry cat asides.

## Settings design language

- **settings visual voice** — quiet companion: a calm, rounded humanist voice that keeps the settings surface warm and approachable while making controls easy to scan.
- **settings atmosphere** — playful toybox: an expressive settings surface that makes customization feel like part of the companion experience.
- **settings shape language** — full toybox: larger rounded surfaces, pill tabs, and pill actions that make customization feel tactile and playful while preserving clear semantic grouping.
- **settings color behavior** — accent on active states: a restrained visual language with neutral surfaces and dark text, where one accent signals selection, enabled controls, and focus.
- **settings accent theme** — butter sun: a soft pastel yellow accent that keeps the toybox mood cheerful while neutral surfaces and dark ink carry the interface structure.
- **settings typography** — system humanist: Segoe UI Variable on Windows 11 (Avenir Next on macOS, common UI sans on Linux), semibold display for titles and shelves, regular text for copy.
- **settings motion** — toybox snap: a lively cat loop with small lift feedback on interactive controls, kept quick enough that the settings surface still feels responsive. Hover lifts ease in over about 120ms and switches glide over about 170ms on a decelerating curve; both jump straight to their end state when the system turns animations off.
- **settings button treatment** — soft toybox pill: navigation, choices, and actions share one rounded face, warm hairline border, soft ambient shadow that lifts on hover, Butter selected state, and a small pressed shift. Navigation items stay flush with the rail until hovered or selected.
- **settings surface depth** — soft shelf: shelves use a hairline warm border and a faint diffuse shadow rather than heavy outlines; the scrollbar is a slim neutral thumb so Butter Sun stays reserved for active states.
- **settings spacing** — roomy playroom: generous spacing that lets each settings group feel like a distinct object while keeping the page calm to scan.
- **settings navigation** — toy rail: a stable vertical rail keeps Settings, Metrics, Wardrobe, and Achievements visible while the selected page owns the roomy content area.
- **cat stage** — the large square at the top of the toy rail that shows the current cat and outfit. The rail stays pinned while pages scroll so the cat is always in view; on short windows the stage shrinks before any navigation item is hidden.
- **accessory locker** — the Wardrobe page: every visible accessory as a tile, grouped by slot with filter chips instead of pages. Each slot starts with a **None** tile. Equipped tiles carry the Butter fill and a check mark; locked tiles are faded grey with a padlock and a small progress meter, and choosing one opens its achievement.
- **try-on** — hovering a locker tile, locked ones included, dresses the cat stage in that accessory without saving; the rail subtitle reads "Trying on …" until the pointer leaves.
- **reward album** — the Achievements page: an overall completion meter, an **Up next** spotlight on the locked reward nearest completion, All / In progress / Unlocked filters, and sections for Typing milestones, Active days, and Discoveries. Secret rewards stay out of the album and its totals until earned, then appear under Discoveries.
- **settings section grouping** — toy shelves: distinct rounded sections give Companion, Appearance, Behavior, and other settings groups equal visual weight.
- **settings focus treatment** — Butter + ink ring: a dark inner focus ring with a Butter Sun outer ring keeps keyboard focus unmistakable while fitting the toybox language.
- **settings save feedback** — Quiet inline: auto-save feedback stays as a small persistent status line near the action area, discoverable without interrupting the settings flow or adding transient notification noise.
- **settings save error** — Inline + retry: a compact inline message explains the failure with wording and an icon, then exposes one direct Retry action while keeping the user in context.
- **settings empty state** — Friendly shelf: an empty section gets a small consistent visual anchor, a plain explanation, and one useful next step without becoming a promotional card grid.

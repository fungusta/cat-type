# Cat Type language

## Product

- **Cat Type** — the desktop companion that appears while someone types.
- **typing companion** — the animated cat experience; it reacts to keyboard activity and fades after typing stops.
- **cat style** — one of the six visual cats: gray, ginger, charcoal, brown-tabby, white, or black-and-white.
- **wardrobe** — the collection of equippable accessories that customize every cat.
- **achievement** — a permanent reward earned through typing activity or active days.

## Website intent

- **new user** — a person discovering Cat Type who needs to understand the charm quickly and reach a platform download.
- **warm & companionable** — the chosen visual direction: cream, paper, terracotta, and forest green with an authored desk-object feeling.
- **product proof** — real Cat Type artwork and believable app details that show the experience rather than relying on generic marketing imagery.
- **homepage promise** — watch the cat move, then customize it; typing behavior supports this promise instead of leading it.
- **motion approach** — a tiny typing window using the existing flat cat artwork; the page does not need three.js or a 3D scene.
- **customization interaction** — visitors press **Mix it up** to vary the outfit; they do not need to manage accessory choices on the landing page.
- **copy voice** — calm product language with occasional dry cat asides.

# Cat Type v1.0.57

This release gives every cat a nose and brings back Leo's eye rings.

## What's new

- **Every cat has a nose.** A small nose now sits above each cat's mouth,
  colored to suit its coat. Mushroom's matches his toe beans, Peanut's is dark
  grey like the real cat's, and the others are shades of pink.
- **Leo's eye rings are back.** Leo has pale cream rings around his eyes again,
  with his dark tabby eye lines running out from them.

## Downloads and updates

Use **Check for updates** in Cat Type, or quit the app before updating manually.

Cat Type v1.0.36 adds a Wardrobe with hats and glasses you earn by typing.

- Open **Settings → Wardrobe** to preview and equip accessories. One hat and one pair of glasses can be worn together across all six cats, including **Mix it up**.
- Unlock **Round glasses**, **Beanie**, **Star glasses**, **Party hat**, and **Crown** at 1,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000, and 100,000 total keystrokes.
- Earn **Sunglasses** by typing on seven different days; they do not need to be consecutive.
- Existing typing history counts toward rewards. Unlocks are permanent and saved locally, with live progress and a small notification when a new accessory is earned.
- Preview your outfit, then choose **Save changes** to apply it. Choose **None** to remove a hat or glasses.

Includes Windows x64, macOS Intel and Apple Silicon, and Linux x64 and ARM64 builds. The macOS DMGs are signed and notarized. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for all five downloads.

# Cat Type v1.0.56

This release gives the cats their names and redraws two of them to look like
the real cats they're based on.

## What's new

- **Every cat has a name.** The cat style picker now shows Mushroom, Donut,
  Peanut, Leo, Maru, and Allie instead of coat colors. Your saved choice
  carries over.
- **Peanut, redrawn.** Peanut is now a soft slate-blue with a peach streak down
  her forehead and a pale chin, instead of a near-black cat.
- **Leo, redrawn.** Leo now has a proper tabby "M" on his forehead, dark lines
  from the corners of his eyes, tapered stripes, a warmer brown coat, and a
  little pink nose.

## Downloads and updates

Use **Check for updates** in Cat Type, or quit the app before updating manually.

# Cat Type v1.0.53

This release redesigns the Wardrobe and Achievements pages, makes every
settings page easier to read, and lets you resize the settings window.
It supersedes v1.0.51 and v1.0.52, which were never published.

## What's new

- **Wardrobe is now an accessory locker.** Every accessory appears at once as a
  tile, grouped by slot, with filters for Hats, Glasses, Neckwear, Back, and
  Ears. Click a tile to wear it, or choose **None** to clear that slot. Earned
  accessories have a short description; locked ones are greyed out with a
  padlock and show how close you are to earning them.
- **Try before you wear.** Hover any tile, locked ones included, to see it on
  your cat in the side rail. Nothing changes until you click.
- **Achievements is now a reward album.** A completion bar shows your progress
  overall, **Up next** highlights the reward you're closest to, and filters let
  you show everything, what's in progress, or what you've unlocked. Rewards are
  grouped into typing milestones, active days, and discoveries, each with its
  own progress bar.
- **Easier to read.** Text throughout settings is larger with stronger
  contrast, Metrics shows today, the last 7 days, and all time as big numbers,
  and the side rail has an icon for each page.
- **Resizable window.** Drag any edge or corner of the settings window to
  resize it. On narrow windows the side rail shrinks to icons so pages keep
  their room.

## Fixes

- The "Saved!" confirmation no longer breaks onto two lines.
- Dragging the settings window by its title bar no longer risks closing the app.

## Downloads and updates

Use **Check for updates** in Cat Type, or quit the app before updating manually.

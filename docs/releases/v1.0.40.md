# Cat Type v1.0.40

The wardrobe now has more ways to celebrate your typing, with 15 new rewards
and a cleaner fit for the cape accessories.

## What's new

- Added 12 public achievements: Ribbon clip, Bell collar, Leaf sprout, Travel
  satchel, Pixel glasses, Wizard hat, Dragon wings, Royal cape, Daisy clip,
  Aviator goggles, Sailor hat, and Laurel wreath.
- Added three secret achievements with new accessories to discover.
- Public milestones now span 250 to 2,000,000 keystrokes and 3 to 365 active
  days. Existing history counts automatically, and rewards remain permanent.
- Reworked Adventure cape and Royal cape into short shoulder capes with neck
  fastenings that follow each cat's cropped silhouette. Their animated paws
  remain in front.
- The Bell collar now wraps around each cat's neck with smooth, fitted edges.
- Added editable SVG artwork guidance for hats, glasses, ears, neckwear, and
  layered back accessories.

Your existing preferences, activity counts, outfits, and earned rewards carry
forward. This is a stable release.

## Downloads and updates

- **Windows x64:** download and run `Cat-Type-Windows-x64.exe`.
- **macOS:** choose the signed and notarized DMG for Intel (`x64`) or Apple silicon (`arm64`).
- **Linux:** extract the archive matching your architecture and run `Cat Type`.

Use **Check for updates** in Cat Type, or quit the app before updating manually.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for all five downloads.

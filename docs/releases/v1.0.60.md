# Cat Type v1.0.60

This release adds twelve new accessories to unlock, brings unlock notices
on Mac into Cat Type's own name, and smooths several parts of the settings
window. It supersedes v1.0.59, which was never published.

## What's new

- **Twelve new accessories.** Unlock a fishing hat, chef's hat, cowboy hat,
  frog hat, suit and tie, tuxedo, flower lei, fish necklace, fishing rod,
  superhero cape, heart glasses, and headphones. Most are typing milestones
  between 7,500 and 750,000 keystrokes; the fishing hat, flower lei, fishing
  rod, and cowboy hat reward days spent typing together.
- **Unlock notices show off the reward.** On Mac, a new unlock now arrives as
  a Cat Type notification with a picture of your cat wearing what it just
  earned. macOS asks once whether Cat Type may send notifications.
- **A clearer "NEW" badge.** When there's something new in Achievements, the
  navigation shows an orange NEW badge instead of extra words in the label.
- **Settings polish.** On Mac, buttons show the standard pointing-hand
  cursor, pressing a button no longer flashes a dark edge, and the "Saved!"
  pill has smooth rounded corners. The rail now shows just your cat above the
  navigation.

## Downloads and updates

Use **Check for updates** in Cat Type, or quit the app before updating manually.
